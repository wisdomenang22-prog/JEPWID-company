We start by understanding the requirement, defining a practical scope and sourcing the right resources. Clear communication, cost awareness, quality control and responsible documentation guide every engagement.

What we do

Capability for every step forward.

Integrated services that support organizations from planning and procurement through execution and ongoing operations.

01 / Build

Concrete products manufacturing

Concrete blocks in 9", 6", 5" and 4" sizes, plus ring and rectangular culverts, kerbs and interlocking stones. Factory at Mbiabong, Uyo LGA.

02 / Trade

Wholesale & retail trade

Domestic goods, foodstuffs and manufactured goods, with commodity exports including rubber, palm oil, cashew nuts and dried fish.

03 / Welcome

Hospitality & tourism

Jessy Hotel and Resort in Ikot Abasi, with a one-star hotel under construction in Uyo near Ibom Tropicana Entertainment Centre.

04 / Construct

Construction & civil engineering

Planning and design, structures, building and infrastructure repair, property maintenance, and wastewater and sewage systems.

05 / Supply

Petroleum products distribution

Developing a distribution network for AGO, PMS, kerosene, LPG cooking gas and lubricants through our subsidiary.

06 / Provide

Borehole drilling

Seismic investigations, design and domestic or industrial borehole drilling for individuals and companies.
